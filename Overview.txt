1. AI SOLUTION
Mental health prediction is a critical task in healthcare analytics and affective computing. Assessing mental health status from behavioral and physiological data takes time and requires many resources and expertise. Researchers and healthcare providers usually assess mental health through clinical interviews, standardized questionnaires, or expensive proprietary systems. This manual assessment is subjective, time-consuming, and especially challenging in places that do not have many resources. Small research groups and startups find it hard to implement mental health prediction quickly because they do not have access to specialized systems or computational resources.

We plan to use the mental_health_multimodal dataset. This dataset contains 4,000 samples with 21 features (3 clinical scores and 18 behavioral/physiological features) and 4 mental health status categories. The features capture digital biomarkers from user interactions, facial expressions, voice analysis, and physiological signals. We will build a tool that helps researchers, developers, and healthcare professionals detect and classify mental health status. The tool uses a 1D Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) for classification. It can recognize 4 mental health status categories: Healthy, Mild_Stress, Moderate_Stress, and Severe_Stress. This AI solution matches global goals of using AI technologies to build new tools for healthcare analytics and mental health monitoring.

The solution will lower the computational resources needed. It will give a path from feature analysis to mental health status prediction for applications in healthcare decision support, mental health monitoring, and user experience design. It will also bring mental health assessment technology to researchers and developers who cannot afford or access specialized systems.

3. BUSINESS SUCCESS CRITERIA

A. Accuracy
Model C Results: The 1D CNN model (Model C) uses all 21 features (3 scores + 18 behavioral/physiological) to predict mental health status. The model achieves reasonable accuracy on the test set. Comprehensive evaluation metrics including accuracy, macro F1, weighted F1, and per-class precision/recall are documented in the experiment report.

B. Time Efficiency
The system needs to process feature data and create an analysis report, including classification, confidence scores and probabilities for all classes efficiently using standard computational resources.

C. Confidence Score
The system must give confidence scores (probabilities) for each prediction along with a reliability assessment so users understand how certain the mental health status classification is.

D. Actionable Insights
The system must provide mental health status information including typical feature patterns, use cases, and potential applications so developers can take immediate action in their applications.

E. Accessibility
The system must be easy to use for non-technical people through simple interfaces (command-line, programmatic API) and work without internet after the model is trained to help remote areas that have no connectivity.

4. BUSINESS BACKGROUND
Academic institutions, startups, and developing regions have big problems in accessing mental health assessment technology, one of the most serious being the very high cost and limited availability of specialized systems. The people who are working in these roles often depend on proprietary software or expensive hardware with limited computational resources. This leads to barriers in implementing mental health prediction in healthcare systems, mental health monitoring applications, and user experience research. Small research groups and startups, who constitute the majority of innovators in many regions, can't get or afford specialized mental health assessment systems. They depend on manual methods or wait for expensive solutions, which often comes too late. Mental health conditions such as Severe_Stress are particularly hard to detect due to class imbalance and lack of predictive signal in behavioral features, which can limit the effectiveness of applications that rely on accurate mental health assessment.

MENTAL HEALTH PREDICTION saw these problems. Created an AI platform made to help reduce the barrier to entry for mental health assessment by automatically analyzing behavioral and physiological features and classifying them into 4 categories. It uses Deep Learning (1D Convolutional Neural Network) to determine the mental health status, trained from scratch without pretrained models. This automatic process helps with classification and mental health status detection efficiently. It is a tool that gives researchers, developers, and healthcare professionals confidence when working with mental health assessment, allowing them to act quickly and implement mental health-aware systems.
